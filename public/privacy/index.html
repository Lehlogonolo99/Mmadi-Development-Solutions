Privacy notice

How we handle the personal information you give us, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Who we are

Mmadi Development Solutions (Pty) Ltd, registration number 2026/580350/07, Stand No 10321, Ga-Moretsele Village, Jane Furse, 1085. We are the responsible party for the information described here.

What we collect and why

When you book a repair, book at the café, apply for a learnership or send us a message, we collect what you type into the form: usually your name, phone number, email, and the details of your request or application. We use it only to handle that request, contact you about it, and, for learnership applications, to assess your application and apply for funding for an intake.

Who sees it

Only our employees, who sign in to a staff system to see it. Our website and data are hosted on Google Firebase. We don’t sell your information or use it for advertising. For learnerships, shortlisted applicants’ details may be shared with MICT SETA as part of a funding application.

How long we keep it

Repair and café records are kept for as long as we need them for warranty, accounting and legal reasons. Learnership applications are kept while intakes are being filled, unless you ask us to remove yours.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by contacting us. You can also complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa.
