What we fix

Phone repairs

Screens, batteries, charging ports, speakers, microphones and cameras, for Android phones and iPhones.

Laptop and PC repairs

Slow machines, broken screens and keyboards, failed hard drives, overheating, and upgrades to SSD and more memory.

Software installs and updates

Windows and Office setup, updates, antivirus, drivers, data backup, and moving your files to a new device.

Data recovery

Getting photos and documents off a phone or laptop that won’t start, where the storage can still be read.

Unlocking phones and accounts

Forgot your PIN or pattern, or bought a phone still linked to the previous owner’s Google or Apple account? We help you get back in.

We only unlock devices with proof of ownership. Bring your ID and a receipt, contract, or a signed letter from the previous owner. We don’t unlock devices that are reported lost or stolen.
