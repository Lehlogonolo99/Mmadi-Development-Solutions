IT learnerships

A year of paid, hands-on IT work for young people, funded through MICT SETA. You finish with a MICT SETA certificate and industry certifications from Microsoft and AWS.

Apply now Check your application

How it works

  1. Apply

    Apply to an open intake, or to our general pool if none is open.

  2. Selection and funding

    We shortlist and interview candidates, then apply to MICT SETA for funding.

  3. Learn on the job

    Classroom modules plus real work in our repair shop and café, with a monthly stipend.

  4. Get certified

    Write foundation exams along the way and receive your MICT SETA certificate at the end.

Certifications you work towards

Each intake lists the exact qualifications it covers.

  • MICT SETA certificate for the accredited learnership qualification.
  • Microsoft Azure Fundamentals (AZ-900) and Microsoft 365 Fundamentals.
  • AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner, the entry certificate for Amazon Web Services.
